Original isolated episodes · One correction per family · Mug handoff

In an original isolated episode the robot meets one person with a specified preference. When its first action already matched that preference, a separate contrast episode tests a person whose profile disagrees with its first choice. The contrast cases are selected examples of correction.

In the continuous series the same robot and person keep their conversations across all ten tasks. On each probe a fresh cold robot sees the task and image. A second fresh robot also gets the written note. The episode learner keeps its conversation and can ask a question before acting.
