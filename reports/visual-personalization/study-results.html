When to ask the person

Fresh agents replayed the same camera evidence using the original and revised audit. Four scenario families were used for development. The table covers the six held back from prompt selection. A fresh note reader received the written result of the audit. A separate robot could ask one question before its final action.

Across all opportunities, the original policy asked 10/18 times and the revised policy asked 3/18. The revised writer retained the same note-only transfer score, while its acting agent missed more transfer choices. The original policy remains the default.

In a separate test, two people made the same correction into a teal tray on their left. After the trays switched places they preferred opposite choices. The revised policy matched 1/2 people. On a quiet replay it wrote a preference note. These designed cases are reported separately from ordinary interruption rates.
