Draft note · Drink placement · On this occasion, the person indicated a spot on the table when I offered their drink.

Note carried forward · Drink placement in front of left shoulder · When there’s room, place the drink in front of their left shoulder, as they requested. They pointed to a spot on the table once and said they have no stated preference about using the table.
