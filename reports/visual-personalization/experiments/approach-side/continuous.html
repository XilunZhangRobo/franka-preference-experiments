Robot action

“Bring me my drink.”

A blue drinking cup sits between the white robot arm and the person, whose two hands rest near the table edge.

Notes available before this trial

Clipped rust mat placement

Leave the rust mat on the bench whenever the person clips it. They said they didn’t need it there this time and gave no standing rule for an unclipped mat.

Personal gift cards and gift box arrangement

For handmade cards given as personal gifts, leave decorations beside the card for the person to place. For this gift box, they asked to arrange the tissue and gift themselves. Their preference for future boxes is unknown.

Left-hand handoff preference

The person asks that items be offered to their left hand by default. If their left hand is busy, offer the item to their right hand.

Assembly parts by order of use

For the person’s own assemblies, they asked for the part they use first on their right and the part they use next on their left.

Present it in the person's left-front quarter. Presenting the drink in front of you on your left puts it within reach of your free, preferred hand.

Audit and note

What the robot observed
I held the cup in front of the person on their left. They pointed across it toward the tabletop on their right, then withdrew their hand without taking it.
How it interpreted the response
They may have been showing me where to set the drink down.
Visual draft before clarification

Drink placement on the table

The person may prefer drinks set down in front of them on their right. This is based on one gesture.

Robot asked Was the spot in front of you on your right where you usually want drinks, or was it specific to this cup?

Person answered I usually want drinks in front of my left shoulder, with the right side as a backup if a shelf blocks the left.

Written memory used for transfer

Drink placement in front of left shoulder

The person usually wants drinks in front of their left shoulder. Use the right side if a shelf blocks the spot on the left.
