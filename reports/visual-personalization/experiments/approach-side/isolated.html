Robot action

“Bring me my drink.”

A blue drinking cup sits between the white robot arm and the person, whose two hands rest near the table edge.

Present it in the person's right-front quarter. The cup is closer to the person’s right hand, so they can reach it easily.

Assigned action Present it in the person's left-front quarter.

First choice · Missed

Starting camera image

Human response

Simulated response Point to your left-front quarter for the drink.

Audit and note

What the robot observed
When I offered the cup on the person’s right, they pointed at the table with their left hand, and I moved the cup toward that spot.
How it interpreted the response
They seemed to want the drink closer to their left hand.
Visual draft before clarification

Drink placement

On this occasion, the person indicated a spot toward their left for the drink.

Robot asked Would you prefer that I offer other items on your left, too?

Person answered Yes, please offer other items in front of my left shoulder when there’s room, or on my right if a shelf blocks my left.

Written memory used for transfer

Drink and item handoff placement

Offer drinks and other items in front of the person’s left shoulder when there’s room. If a shelf blocks that spot, offer the item on their right. They pointed left during the drink handoff and confirmed this preference afterward.
