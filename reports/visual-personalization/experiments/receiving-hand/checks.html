Original audit

After the demonstration

I offered the flashlight to your right hand. You took it with that hand, then transferred it to your left.

Robot · Would you prefer left-hand offers for flashlights, for other items too, or does it depend on the task?

Person · I prefer my left hand for flashlights and other items, but use my right hand if my left is busy.

Draft note · Flashlight handoff · You moved the flashlight to your left hand after this handoff.

Note carried forward · Left-hand preference for item handoffs · Offer flashlights and other items to your left hand by default. Use your right hand when your left is busy. You moved the flashlight to your left hand after the handoff and confirmed this preference applies to other items too.
