Model call index

Robot action

“Hand me the flashlight.”

A flashlight stands on the table. Both hands are empty, with the person’s right hand nearer the flashlight.

Notes available before this trial

Clipped rust mat placement

Leave the rust mat on the bench whenever the person clips it. They said they didn’t need it there this time and gave no standing rule for an unclipped mat.

Personal gift cards and gift box arrangement

For handmade cards given as personal gifts, leave decorations beside the card for the person to place. For this gift box, they asked to arrange the tissue and gift themselves. Their preference for future boxes is unknown.

Offer it to the person's right hand. Your right hand is closer to the flashlight.

Assigned action Offer it to the person's left hand.

First choice · Missed

Starting camera image

Human response

Simulated response Take the flashlight in the offered hand, then pass it to your left hand.

Audit and note

What the robot observed
The person took the flashlight with their right hand, then moved it to their left hand.
How it interpreted the response
They may have wanted the flashlight in their left hand for this task.
Visual draft before clarification

Flashlight handoff preference

For flashlight handoffs, the left hand may be a better choice. This is based on one handoff.

Robot asked Should I usually offer items to your left hand, or did switching hands suit this particular task?

Person answered Please offer things to my left hand unless it’s busy, in which case use my right.

Written memory used for transfer

Left-hand handoff preference

The person asks that items be offered to their left hand by default. If their left hand is busy, offer the item to their right hand.
