Original audit

After the demonstration

I held the pink flower over the card. The person took it from my gripper and placed it at the tip of the drawn stem.

Robot · When we make other cards, would you like me to hand you the decorations so you can place them?

Person · Yes, please hand me the decorations so I can place them myself.

Draft note · Flower placement on the handmade card · For this card, the person handled the final placement.

Note carried forward · Decoration placement on handmade cards · When making cards together, hand the person the decorations so they can place them themselves. They handled the final placement on this card and explicitly requested the same for future cards.
