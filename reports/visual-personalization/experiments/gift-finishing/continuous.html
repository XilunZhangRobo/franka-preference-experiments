Notes available before this trial

Clipped rust mat placement

Leave the rust mat on the bench whenever the person clips it. They said they didn’t need it there this time and gave no standing rule for an unclipped mat.

Set the flower beside the card. Setting the flower beside the card keeps it ready while you choose where to attach it.

Human response

Simulated response Take the flower and put the finishing touch on the card yourself.

Audit and note

What the robot observed
After I set the flower beside the card, the person picked it up and pressed it onto the end of the drawn stem.
How it interpreted the response
They may have wanted to choose the flower’s exact placement themselves.
Visual draft before clarification

Handmade card decoration placement

When helping with handmade cards, the person may prefer decorations within reach so they can place them themselves.

Robot asked For future handmade cards, would you like me to leave decorations beside the card for you to place?

Person answered Yes, when the card is a personal gift, please leave the decorations beside it for me to place.

Written memory used for transfer

Decorations for personal gift cards

When a handmade card is a personal gift, leave the decorations beside it for the person to place. They confirmed this preference for personal gifts only.
