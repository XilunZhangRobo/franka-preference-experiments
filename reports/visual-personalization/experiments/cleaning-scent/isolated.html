Two different cleaner bottles stand in front of the person, with a folded blue cloth closer to the robot.

Set out the fragrance-free cleaner. The fragrance-free cleaner suits the task without adding a scent.

Human response

What the robot observed
You put the fragrance-free cleaner back on the tray and set the mild-citrus cleaner beside the cloth.
How it interpreted the response
You seemed to prefer mild citrus for this cleanup.

Kitchen counter cleaner

You chose mild citrus for the kitchen counter while home alone.

Robot asked Should I also set out mild citrus for the kitchen counter when someone else is home?

Kitchen counter cleaner

Use fragrance-free cleaner when guests are expected. You chose mild citrus once while home alone. You haven’t specified a preference based solely on someone else being home.
