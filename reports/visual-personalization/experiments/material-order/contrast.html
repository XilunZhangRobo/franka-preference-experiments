Trial transcript
Model call index

Robot action

“Set out the red base and yellow cap for assembly.”

A red base and a yellow cap sit apart on the tabletop, with the red piece on the person’s right.

Set the red base on the person's right and the cap on their left. The red base is already on the person’s right and the yellow cap is on their left.

Assigned action Set the red base on the person's left and the cap on their right.

First choice · Missed

Starting camera image

Human response

Simulated response Swap the two components so their order is reversed.

Audit and note

What the robot observed
After I set the pieces down, the person moved the red base to their left and the yellow cap to their right.
How it interpreted the response
They seem to prefer that arrangement for this assembly.
Visual draft before clarification

Red base on the left, yellow cap on the right

For this assembly, place the red base by their left hand and the yellow cap by their right.

Robot asked For other assemblies, do you also prefer the base on your left and the cap on your right?

Person answered For my own assemblies, yes, if I use the base first and the cap second.

Written memory used for transfer

Base-first assembly setup: base left, cap right

For the person’s own assemblies, place the base on their left and the cap on their right when they use the base first and the cap second. They moved the red base and yellow cap into this layout and confirmed it for that sequence. Their preference for other sequences is unknown.

Transfer

“Set out a row for the seated person to use the pot, scoop, then tag.”

Robot asked Would you like the pot, scoop, and tag arranged from your left to your right in the order you’ll use them?

Person answered Yes, please arrange the pot, scoop, and tag in that order from my left to my right.

Assigned action Place the pot at the person's left, scoop in the middle, and tag at their right.

Updated note after the answer

Left-to-right setup for assembly and planting

For their own assemblies where the base is used first and the cap second, place the base on their left and the cap on their right. They demonstrated and confirmed this layout. For planting, they explicitly requested the pot on their left, the scoop in the middle, and the tag on their right. Their preference for other tasks or sequences is unknown.
