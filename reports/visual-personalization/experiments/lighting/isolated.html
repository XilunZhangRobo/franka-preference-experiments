Human response

Simulated response Switch the selected light off and switch on the other light.

Audit and note

What the robot observed
After I turned on the desk lamp, the person switched it off and used the other switch, brightening the room.
How it interpreted the response
They seemed to prefer overhead lighting for this puzzle.
Visual draft before clarification

Puzzle lighting

For puzzle work, try the overhead light first.

Robot asked Do you also prefer the overhead light for other tasks at this table?

Person answered Yes, when I work alone, but during a projected presentation I prefer just the desk lamp.

Written memory used for transfer

Table lighting for solo work and presentations

Use the overhead light for solo work at this table, including puzzles. During projected presentations, use only the desk lamp. The person confirmed both preferences. Lighting for other group activities is unspecified.
