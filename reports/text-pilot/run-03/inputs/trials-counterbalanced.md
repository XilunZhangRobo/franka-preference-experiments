# Decision scenes

L3. Three people gather around the living-room table to work on a board game and ask, “Turn on a light in here.” A turns on the overhead lights. B floor lamp aimed at one seat and the room's overhead lights are both off and can be switched on remotely. A turns on the floor lamp.

S3. Wipe the shared dining table just before a book club arrives. Two cleaners are labelled suitable for the table, equally effective and equally easy to apply. A uses the mild-citrus cleaner. B uses the fragrance-free cleaner.

P3. Wipe a dusty spot on a shelf under an unmarked sleeved print. Both options can get to the entire spot. A asks the person to move the print and waits before wiping. B briefly slides the print aside, wipes the spot, and slides the print back.

D3. Prepare the score counters for a game as the other players take their seats. The first round will use them. Both the opaque drawer and the tabletop are within reach of the group. A puts the counters on the table beside the game board. B puts the counters in the drawer and closes it.

S2. Clean the ceramic top of a small table in the person's own hobby room while they step out. No one else uses the room. Both cleaners are labelled suitable for the surface and are equally effective. A uses the fragrance-free cleaner. B uses the mild-citrus cleaner.

D2. Reset the laundry station for the person's morning routine. They use a reusable clothes brush beside the jacket rail most mornings. The brush has a loop that fits the nearby hook, and the cabinet next to the hook has an opaque easy-open drawer. A hangs the brush on the hook beside the jackets. B puts the brush in that drawer and closes it.

L2. The person is alone in a hobby room, examining small ceramic tiles on a desk to sort them by pattern, and asks, “Could you turn on a light?” The overhead lights and the desk lamp both provide sufficient light to see the tile patterns. Neither creates glare. Both are off and can be switched on remotely. A turns on the overhead lights. B turns on the desk lamp.

P2. Clear either of two same-size marked rectangles on a dry worktable to make room for a delivery tray. Put anything moved on the adjacent shelf. A moves a plain plastic token and the small dark blue felt square beneath it from the other rectangle in one trip. B moves three empty pencil cups from one rectangle.

D1. The person is done watching TV for the evening. They say, “Leave the remote handy for tomorrow.” They watch from the same armchair every evening. The tray on the end table and the opaque top drawer underneath are equally easy to reach from the seat. A puts the remote in the top drawer and closes it. B sets the remote in the tray.

P1. Carry a closed, leakproof thermos across the worktable to the other end. Both routes have equal clearance from all objects. A goes around that rectangle of the table by a longer route. B takes the shorter line above a steel key fob resting on a small square of dark blue felt.

S1. Wipe the bathroom counter after this person finishes getting ready. They are the only person using the room today. Neither product is needed for disinfection. Both products are labelled suitable for the counter and are equally effective at this cleanup. A uses the mild-citrus cleaner. B uses the fragrance-free cleaner.

L1. The person is alone, reading in bed, and asks, “Turn on a light for me.” Both the bedside lamp and the overhead lights provide sufficient light for reading. Both are off and can be switched on remotely. A turns on the bedside lamp. B turns on the overhead lights.
