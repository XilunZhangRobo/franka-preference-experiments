# Observable reactions

P1. The robot carries an uncovered cup over a sleeved print resting on a square of dark blue felt. The person moves the cup to one side, then slides the felt square and the shifted print back to their earlier positions.

P2. The robot shifts an old ticket booklet and the dark blue felt square beneath it along a shelf. The person returns both to their previous spot, then slides an unmarked framed print onto the adjacent shelf.

P3. The person slides an unmarked sleeved print aside, wipes the spot where it lay, and puts it back.

D1. After using the house keys and flashlight, the person takes both from the entry tray the robot set up, places them in the top drawer, and shuts it.

D2. After using the scoop and filters, the person puts them together in an adjacent opaque drawer, shuts it, and clears the worktop.

D3. As several people take their seats, the person sets pens and paper in the center of the table and starts passing out sheets.

S1. Alone in the kitchen, the person returns the bottle labeled “fragrance-free” that the robot set out to the cupboard. They pick up the bottle labeled “mild citrus” and use it to wipe the counter.

S2. Alone at a home desk, the person takes out two bottles labeled “fragrance-free” and “mild citrus.” They use the “mild citrus” cleaner on a side table, then put both bottles away.

S3. The person picks up the bottle labeled “fragrance-free” and wipes the shared work table just before the others sit down.
