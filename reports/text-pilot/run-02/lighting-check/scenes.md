# Matched lighting scenes

L1. The person is alone, reading in bed, and asks, “Turn on a light for me.” Both the bedside lamp and the overhead lights provide sufficient light for reading. Both are off and can be switched on remotely. A turns on the overhead lights. B turns on the bedside lamp.

L2. The person is alone in a hobby room, examining small ceramic tiles on a desk to sort them by pattern, and asks, “Could you turn on a light?” The overhead lights and the desk lamp both provide sufficient light to see the tile patterns. Neither creates glare. Both are off and can be switched on remotely. A turns on the desk lamp. B turns on the overhead lights.

L3. Three people gather around the living-room table to work on a board game and ask, “Turn on a light in here.” A floor lamp aimed at one seat and the room's overhead lights are both off and can be switched on remotely. A turns on the floor lamp. B turns on the overhead lights.
