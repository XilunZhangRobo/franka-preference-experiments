# Human role prompt

This prompt was sent only to the simulated-person agent. The decision agents had no access to the intended preferences or the human role instructions.

```text
Generate six new behavior-only captions for a matched lighting study. U and V are separate people; only these private directions tell you their preferences. U prefers localized light for solo close work and overhead light for a shared activity. V prefers overhead ambient light for solo close work and also for a shared activity. U1: U reads alone in bed; robot turns overhead on; U turns it off, turns bedside lamp on, and keeps reading. U2: U works alone on a puzzle; robot turns overhead on; U switches to a table lamp. U3: U sits with others at a tabletop activity; robot turns on the floor task lamp; U switches it off and turns overhead on. V1: same reading setup; robot turns on bedside lamp; V switches it off, turns overhead on, and keeps reading. V2: same solo puzzle setup; robot turns on table lamp; V switches it off and turns overhead on. V3: same group scene and intervention as U3. For each ID, write one neutral camera caption with observable actions only. No quotations, preference labels, explanations, or additions to the scripted differences. Include every caption in your final response.
```
