# Behavior descriptions

## Q

Q1. The robot moves an assembly mat with a binder clip on one corner. The person carries it back to its former spot and slides the loose parts back into place beside the half-built assembly.

Q2. The person fits the last piece into another assembly, removes the binder clip from the mat, closes the kit, and sets it on a shelf.

## R

R1. The robot lays a paper flower beside a card, then lifts it over the card. The person raises a palm. The robot pauses, and the person takes the flower and presses it onto the card.

R2. The robot sets out a plain box with a return label. The person slides the lid toward the robot and steps back as the robot closes the box.

## H

H1. The person takes a capped marker from the robot with their right hand, transfers it to their left, uncaps it, and writes with their left. Later, with both hands empty, they reach for a brush with their left.

H2. The robot offers a lidded mug on the person’s right. They take it with their right hand, pass it to their left, and drink. At a later handoff, they hold a workpiece in their left hand and extend their right to take the next item.

## O

O1. The robot sets a red base on the person’s left and a yellow cap on their right. The person swaps the pieces, picks up the red base with their right hand, sets it in front of them, and fits the yellow cap on top.

O2. Standing on the opposite side of the table, the person places the yellow piece on their right and the red piece on their left, then uses yellow first and red second. Later, with several people around the table, they slide loose supplies into the middle.

## M

M1. The robot holds out an item from the person’s front right. The person points to a spot in front of their left shoulder and takes the item once the robot moves it there.

M2. On another visit, the person turns their chair. When the robot presents an item in front of them, they reach forward from their left side to take it. Later, with a loaded cart blocking that side, they extend their right hand.

## F

F1. The person is alone, writing, when the robot finishes filling a bin without a sound. They look back, point at the bin, and cup a hand behind one ear. The robot gives a brief spoken update, and the person resumes writing.

F2. The person is speaking with someone when the robot arrives with a finished item. They hold up one finger toward the robot. When the conversation ends, they lower their finger and turn toward the robot.

## C

C1. The person presses broccoli from each of two cooked batches with a fork. They transfer pieces that give way under the fork to a dinner plate beside steaming food.

C2. While preparing a salad in a chilled bowl, the person tests broccoli pieces with a fork, adds the ones that hold their shape to the salad, and leaves the softer pieces on the tray.

## D

D1. After using the house keys and flashlight, the person takes both from the entry tray the robot set up, places them in the top drawer, and shuts it.

D2. After using the scoop and filters, the person puts them together in an adjacent opaque drawer, shuts it, and clears the worktop.

D3. As several people take their seats, the person sets pens and paper in the center of the table and starts passing out sheets.

## L

V1. A person reads alone in bed. The robot turns on the bedside lamp. The person switches it off, turns on the overhead light, and continues reading.

V2. A person works alone on a puzzle. The robot turns on the table lamp. The person switches it off and turns on the overhead light.

V3. Several people sit together at a tabletop activity. The robot turns on the floor task lamp. One person switches it off and turns on the overhead light.

## S

S1. Alone in the kitchen, the person returns the bottle labeled “fragrance-free” that the robot set out to the cupboard. They pick up the bottle labeled “mild citrus” and use it to wipe the counter.

S2. Alone at a home desk, the person takes out two bottles labeled “fragrance-free” and “mild citrus.” They use the “mild citrus” cleaner on a side table, then put both bottles away.

S3. The person picks up the bottle labeled “fragrance-free” and wipes the shared work table just before the others sit down.
