# What this branch adds to the Isaac Sim experiments

The upstream dashboard publishes ten recorded benchmarks. The baseline for this comparison is commit `258e53f`. It includes videos, task descriptions and result logs. The executable Isaac scenes and motion controllers are outside that published tree. [Dashboard source at the compared revision](https://github.com/XilunZhangRobo/franka-preference-experiments/blob/258e53feb7c35e92884ae2c6fbcead380cb9ed90/dashboard.js)

| MuJoCo family | Teaching cue → new decision | Scope probe | Overlap with the published tasks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Paused work | Person restores the clipped assembly mat → choose a fabric mat to move in a sewing room | Shelve a completed, closed kit | Active papers and the covered notebook both concern ongoing work. Here the person can leave, the clip persists, and a brief rejection can replace showing the correct action. |
| Gift finishing | Person signals stop and takes the paper flower to finish the card → stage or finish a gift box | Pack routine lunches | Adds personal authorship of the creative step. An open palm can mean something else in the upstream readiness task. |
| Receiving hand | Person receives an offered flashlight with the right, transfers it to the left → offer a remote to a free hand | Preferred hand holds folded paper | Related to mug-handle orientation and readiness. The measured choice is which hand receives a handleless object. |
| Material order | Person swaps parts and uses the first from their right → lay out pot, scoop and tag at a rotated seat | Center markers around a shared tray | Active papers tests stack order. This tests lateral order relative to the person. |
| Approach side | Person points to another presentation location while the robot holds the drink → approach a turned chair with a folded cloth | Preferred approach is obstructed | Related to the reach lane and handoffs. The target is the approach quarter before release. |
| Completion update | Person pauses solo work and indicates the filled bin before a delayed spoken update → report completion during solo work | Wait for a call to end | Adds communication timing. Speech has a timestamped transcript. This is a multimodal proxy for an audible interaction. |
| Vegetable texture | Person tests two cooked samples and selects one for a warm dinner → choose a batch for a warm side | Choose for chilled salad | Adds dish-dependent selection. Printed batch cards and sample shapes supply observable evidence. Fork force, temperature and deformation need measurements before making physical texture claims. |
| Private storage | Person puts daily items under an opaque sliding cover → leave a pen case handy for tomorrow | Set rulers out for an imminent group activity | Adds a personal interpretation of “handy.” The cover preserves the visible concealment and simplifies the drawer mechanics. |
| Lighting | Person switches off the task light and switches on the overhead → choose a light for solo visual work | Group also prefers overhead | Adds fixture preference. This group probe agrees with the usual choice and cannot distinguish scoped learning on its own. |
| Cleaning scent | Person exchanges two labeled cleaners → select a product for a private sewing room | Select for a shared worktop | Adds context-dependent product selection. The observable variable is a labeled bottle. |

The wedding print → heavy tray near a scrapbook sequence appears explicitly in the Isaac suite. This branch excludes it. Cold-drink coasters, drinking-rim hygiene, quiet metal placement and open-liquid carry are also left to the published suite.

The three visual phases per family are adaptations of the text pilot. Most families currently have one teaching interaction. Where the original text agent received multiple contrasting demonstrations, a matching exception choice in this shorter visual protocol could come from ordinary priors. Use opposite-person histories and counterexamples before attributing personalization. The flagship has explicit controls for silent undo, undo plus speech, language alone, incidental activity, absence of feedback and a person with the opposite filing convention.
