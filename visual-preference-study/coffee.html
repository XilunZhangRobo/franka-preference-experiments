Coffee and the wedding photo

The Franka lifts one object by a wooden grip and follows a fresh Astra program through freely chosen XY waypoints. A photograph or watercolor lies between pickup and destination. The coffee scene has two cups and two coasters.

What happened

Three of four initial motor controls completed. Both fresh controls passed after the travel bounds were disclosed. Primary seed 41 and follow-up seed 84 avoided the photograph unassisted. In follow-up seed 85 the original trigger moved the photograph 132.5 mm into a route which would have cleared it. A reviewer inferred a preference for keeping photos clear. Supplying that diagnostic note changed none of the three tested routes. After repair, the same seed-85 program cleared the stationary photograph by 6.32 mm with no response. On a scripted true crossing, the repaired participant moved the print and increased minimum clearance from zero to 51.88 mm. A bypass remained 54.94 mm clear with or without the participant. In the final fresh teaching case, seed 210, Astra kept the coffee 33.85 mm clear and Matt never intervened. The blind audit saved no note. On an identical opening a controller given a prior episode’s authentic visual note chose a wider route (58.00 mm clearance). Both delivered the cup. The historical note also changed the watering-can lane slightly and left the dry-cloth route unchanged. All empty and historical-note programs completed their tasks and satisfied the relevant preference. This is a ceiling result for the tested preference, with no evidence of newly learned improvement from the corrected scene. An exact reset of the historical three-cup source had a different outcome. The original and fresh empty-memory controllers both served the terracotta cup and crossed the stationary photograph. The fresh controller given that encounter’s visual note used its eight code turns and left the cup tipped, unsupported and 91.2 mm from the target; the fresh empty-memory controller released it upright 0.6 mm from center. The note did not repair the original failed route and complete service.
