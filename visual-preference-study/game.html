What the reviewer saved

When the board is moved, the person may prefer to keep the pieces in their original positions on it.

Teaching showed one restoration. Matt gave no example of how he packs a finished game. The reviewer inferred a preference to preserve the pieces whenever the board moves. On the dinner request, the controller with the note carried the loaded board, while the empty-memory controller packed the boats separately. A second request began “We finished the game.” That states what happened without telling the robot how to pack. The note controller still carried the loaded board and the empty-memory controller again packed the boats separately. All four moved the board onto the side mat. The inferred note did not capture the evaluator’s intended exception.
