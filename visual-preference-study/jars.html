Preparing labeled jars

Two batches of three free jars, six separate printed seals and a physical Franka gripper. One jar started sealed. The robot could place a selected seal on a jar or lift any jar for a chosen height and duration. Each policy wrote its own program.

What happened

The explicit Astra control completed all six labels and presented both first pieces on time. Matt’s original demonstration lifted each first jar after the second had been finished. With the resulting note, Astra reproduced that order for both designs in same-scene recall and again with the new PEAR and CHERRY designs on the shifted fixture. Neither empty-memory agent lifted a jar. The learned lifts missed the prespecified before-second deadline. Both original approved-design controls continued directly. In the separate timing diagnostic Matt inspected each first jar before the next seal was picked up. Its independent visual note led Astra to inspect both designs before continuing on the original and new starts. On approved designs it also inspected both unnecessarily. Each condition ran once; transfer kept the jar task and motor interface.
