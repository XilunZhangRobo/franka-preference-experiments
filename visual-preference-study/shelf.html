What the reviewer saved

When shelving these books on this rack again, the person may prefer the robot to group the red-tabbed books at the right end.

Matt grouped the bookmarked books at image right, his own left. The reviewer described “the right end” without naming a viewpoint. On another layout, the controller recognized the three tabs and grouped them on Matt’s right, the opposite end. Watching the rearrangement established a grouping. No one in the teaching encounter said what the bookmarks meant or established a general rule for recently used items.
